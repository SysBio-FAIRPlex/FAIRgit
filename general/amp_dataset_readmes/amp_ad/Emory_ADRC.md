###The Emory ADRC (Emory_ADRC) Brain Bank study
This study is based on 32 postmortem brain tissues from the [Emory Alzheimer’s Disease Research Center](http://alzheimers.emory.edu). This includes 8 cognitively normal (control), 8 Alzheimer’s disease (AD), 4 Parkinson’s disease (PD), 4 Parkinson’s with Lewy Body Dementia (PD/DLB) and 8 amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). The neuropathology assessment adheres to the standardized protocols outlined in the NIA Best Practice Guidelines. The brains were bisected and one hemisphere was cut coronally in 1cm slabs, and each slab frozen between supercooled metal plates. These slabs were stored at -80˚C for proteomics studies. Paraffin-embedded tissue sections from 18 regions were evaluated with a standard battery of histochemical and immunohistochemical stains to identify the pathologies associated with AD and other neurodegenerative conditions. The standard battery included H&E, Bielschowsky, amyloid beta, alpha synuclein, ubiquitin, phosphorylated tau, and phosphorylated TDP43. The diagnosis of AD was made by following the protocol outlined in the 2012 NIA criteria, with standard NACC uniform data set available. The critical parameters are the densities of neuritic plaques, according to CERAD and neurofibrillary tangles according to Braak. The criteria for diagnosis of AD follow the recommendations of CERAD and the NIA-Reagan Institute.
