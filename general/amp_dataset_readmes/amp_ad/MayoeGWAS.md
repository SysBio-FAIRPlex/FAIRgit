###The MayoeGWAS study
All of the subjects for the Mayo eGWAS were participants in the [Mayo LOAD GWAS](https://www.synapse.org/#!Synapse:syn2910256) and come from the MCBB cohort. Brain tissue samples from two different regions were selected: cerebellum (CBE) and temporal cortex (TCX). This study includes subjects with a pathologic diagnosis of AD (N: 197 CBE, 202 TCX) and non-AD controls with other brain pathology unrelated to AD (N: 177 CBE, 197 TCX). Subjects with other neuropathological diagnosis included progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP); Lewy body disease (LBD); corticobasal degeneration (CBD); frontotemporal lobar degeneration (FTLD); multiple system atrophy (MSA) and vascular dementia (VaD). Eighty-five percent of the subjects in the TCX cohort overlapped with those in the CER cohort.
