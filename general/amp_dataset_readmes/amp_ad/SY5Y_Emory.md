### The SY5Y Cell Line Proteomics (SY5Y_Emory) Study
The SY5Y cells were processed for proteomics analysis five days after transduction. There are 24 samples in 3 groups. Group 1 had 3 controls without any over-expression and 3 APP wildtype over-expression. Group 2 had 3 controls without overexpression, 3 TAU wildtype over-expression, and 3 TAU mutant p301s over-expression. Group 3 had 3 controls without overexpression, 3 wildtype U1-70 over-expression, and 3 mutant U1-70 over-expression (missing RRM domain).
