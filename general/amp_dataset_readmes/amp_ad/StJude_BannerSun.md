###The collaborative St. Jude - Banner Sun Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias (StJude_BannerSun) study
The goal of this study is to apply cutting-edge proteomics, genomics and systems biology approaches to understand molecular dysfunction underlying the pathogenesis of Alzheimer’s disease (AD) and detect potential biomarkers. All clinical samples including post-mortem brain tissue, serum, and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) were obtained from the Banner Sun Health Research Institute. TMT based deep proteomics analysis and systems biology-based data integration were performed by St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. Post-mortem neuropathological evaluation was performed based on established criteria (Beach et al., 2015), including postmortem interval, race, age of death, CERAD, Braak, sex, and a series of neuropathological variables.

For a detailed description of the study and data interpretation, see [Deep Multilayer Brain Proteomics Identifies Molecular Networks in Alzheimer’s Disease Progression (Bai et al., **_Neuron _**2020)](https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(19)31058-X). See also the TMT proteomic data from the [MSBB study](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies?Study=syn3159438) for these eight TMT batches (TMT1, TMT3, TMT4, TMT6, TMT8, TMT9, TMT10, and TMT16) that were also part of this manuscript.
