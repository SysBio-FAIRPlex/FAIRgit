# The Diverse Cohorts Harmonization Study (DivCo_HS)

This study presents reprocessed dataset from the Diverse Cohorts project [https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage/StudyDetails?Study=syn51732482](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage/StudyDetails?Study=syn51732482). Joint Variant calls are shared from reprocessed WGS samples in Diverse Cohorts, as well as overlapping AMP 1.0 donors. In addition, RNASeq harmonization results have also been added to this study. This includes raw RNA expression counts as well as normalized matrices and QC results.
