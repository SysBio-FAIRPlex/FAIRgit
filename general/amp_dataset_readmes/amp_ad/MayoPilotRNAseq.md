###The MayoPilotRNAseq study
Gene expression measures were generated, using next-generation RNA sequencing (RNAseq), from temporal cortex RNA samples collected for 96 subjects with Alzheimer's disease (AD) and 96 subjects with progressive supranuclear palsy (PSP). Subjects in this study were selected from those that were analyzed in the [Mayo Clinic brain eGWAS](http://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1002707); therefore genotype and expression array data are also available for these samples. The AD and PSP cohorts underwent library preparation and sequencing independently and should therefore be considered as two separate datasets.
