###The Harvard Brain Tissue Resource Center (HBTRC) study
Late Onset Alzheimer's Disease (LOAD) subjects and nondemented controls were recruited through the Harvard Brain Tissue Research Center. Each subject was diagnosed at intake and each brain underwent an extensive LOAD-related neuropathology examination.  Tissue was collected from three brain regions per sample: dorsolateral prefrontal cortex, visual cortex, and cerebellum.  A total of 1,647 tissue samples from 549 brain samples were collected from 376 LOAD patients and 153 nondemented healthy controls.

**Note**:
This study is 'pre-award data', meaning that data from this study are supplementary to the AMP-AD data content as they were generated independent of the AMP-AD project but outcomes from analysis of these data represent the basis of the Mt. Sinai AMP-AD project.  As such, **not all details of data generation, annotation or analysis are available and no additional data or other content will be provided**. The data provided here, which are made public within AMP-AD by the Mt. Sinai AMP-AD team, include expression array data generated from prefrontal cortex.  These data include genotypes, covariates, and expression array data generated from 463 prefrontal cortex brain samples (310 LOAD, 153 control).  Provided here is only gene expression from the Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex.

The larger study containing this data and additional the brain regions - as well as samples derived form individual diagnosed with Huntington's Disease has been made public in  [GEO - GSE44772](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE44772) in conjunction with the Zhang et al publication [1].
