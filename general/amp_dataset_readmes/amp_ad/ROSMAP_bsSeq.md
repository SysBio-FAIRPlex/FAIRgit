### The Targeted Bisulfite-Seq on 150 Randomly Selected ROSMAP Samples (ROSMAP_bsSeq) Study

To experimentally test the validity of the prediction scores reported by EWASplus, we performed targeted bisulfite sequencing to measure the methylation level at 559 selected CpGs from 150 randomly selected participants from the Religious Orders Study (ROS) or Memory and Aging Project (MAP) cohorts who are representative of both studies and have available brain tissue for bisulfite sequencing. CpGs were selected for independent validation from the top EWASplus predicted sites using a stepwise selection process that prioritized regions with the highest predicted scores that were physically separated by at least 500 bp. For comparison purposes, we also randomly selected CpGs from regions with predicted scores in the lower half but similar physical characteristics (e.g., GC content). Additionally, we targeted CpGs on the array that could serve as positive controls. After quality control, 319 CpGs were analyzed including 31 CpGs on the 450K array identified as AD-associated, 260 off-array CpGs predicted to be AD-associated based on EWASplus, and 28 off-array CpGs predicted to not be AD-associated.

For more information, see [Huang et al. 2021, _Nat. Comm._](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-24710-8#Sec11)
