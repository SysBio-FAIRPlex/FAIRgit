###The Perelman School of Medicine UPenn Proteomics (UPP) study
Brain tissue used in this study was obtained from the autopsy collections of the [University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine Brain Bank](https://www.med.upenn.edu/cndr/biosamples-brainbank.html).  Tissue was from the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex.  Human postmortem tissues were acquired under proper Institutional Review Board (IRB) protocols.  Postmortem neuropathological evaluation of neuritic plaque distribution was performed according to the Consortium to Establish a Registry for Alzheimer’s Disease (CERAD) criteria [1], while extent of spread of neurofibrillary tangle pathology was assessed with the Braak staging system [2].  Other neuropathologic diagnoses were made in accordance with established criteria and guidelines [3, 4].  All case metadata, including age, sex, post-mortem interval, neuropathological criteria, and summary disease status, are provided in metadata associated with the UPP release.
