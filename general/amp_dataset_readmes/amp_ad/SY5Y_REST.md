###The SY5Y REST Model  (SY5Y_REST) Study

To obtain greater insight into the functional consequences of REST induction in the aging brain, we explored REST targets by chromatin immunoprecipitation and deep sequencing (ChIP-seq) in the neural cell line SH-SY5Y, as well as in LiCl treated (to induce REST expression) SH-SY5Y.
