### The RNAseq Harmonization Study

This study is a release of RNA sequencing data that has been processed using a common workflow. The study is an extension of the [rnaSeqReprocessing study](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn9702085), and has data from donors that are also part of the [WGS_Harmonization study](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn22264775). RNAseq data in this study has been aligned to the GRCh38 reference genome.

The input data for the RNAseq_Harmonization study originates from these original studies:
* [The ROSMAP study](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn3219045): These are two longitudinal studies of aging and Alzheimer’s disease with brain donation upon death - The Religious Order Study (ROS) and Memory and Aging Project (MAP) run by [Rush Alzheimer’s Disease Center](https://www.radc.rush.edu/).
* [The ROSMAP Cognitive Resilience study]( https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn22695346): This is a study on a subset of the ROSMAP donors with varying levels of cognitive function and Alzheimer's disease pathology.
* [The MayoRNAseq study]( https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn5550404): This is a study of Alzheimer’s disease and related dementias on donors from the [Mayo Clinic Brain Bank]( https://www.mayo.edu/research/departments-divisions/department-neuroscience-florida/brain-banks/mayo-clinic-brain-bank) and [Banner Health](https://www.bannerhealth.com/services/research/locations/sun-health-institute/programs/body-donation/tissue)
* [The MSBB study]( https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn3159438): This is a study of Alzheimer’s disease on donors from the [Mount Sinai/JJ Peters VA Medical Center NIH Brain and Tissue Repository]( https://icahn.mssm.edu/research/nih-brain-tissue-repository)
* [The rnaSeqSampleSwap study]( https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn5550419): This is a technical study to evaluate  library prep and sequencing protocols of data originating from the ROSMAP, MayoRNAseq, and MSBB study.
