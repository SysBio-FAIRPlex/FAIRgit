# The CUIMC2 Multiome snRNA/snATAC (CUIMC2_Mult) Study

The Columbia University Medical Center’s (CUIMC) single nucleus multiomic cohort consisted of  240 participants from the Religious Orders Study and Memory Aging Project (ROSMAP) , which is a longitudinal clinical-pathologic cohort of aging and Alzheimer’s disease with participants from two prospective studies of The Religious Order Study (ROS) (N=96) and the Memory and Aging Project (MAP) (N=144). All the participants were of non-Hispanic Caucasian descent, with 89 males (38%), median age at death of 89.79 years (SD=6.75), and median post mortem interval of 6.44 hours (SD=5.21). Alzheimer’s disease related phenotypes such as amyloid, tangles, cognitive decline, and APOE ε4 genotype measurements as well as non-AD related neurological measurements can be additionally requested from the RADC Research Sharing Hub (https://www.radc.rush.edu).
