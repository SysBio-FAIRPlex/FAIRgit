### The Mitochondrial DNA in Aging and AD (mtDNA_AD) Study

Whole genome sequencing (WGS) data from three studies were included: the Religious Orders Study and the Rush Memory and Aging Project (ROSMAP), the Mayo RNA-seq study (Mayo), and the Mount Sinai Brain Bank study (MSBB) [1-3]. In total, n=454 samples from the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (ROSMAP), n=66 samples from the posterior cingulate cortex (ROSMAP), n=242 samples from the cerebellum (ROSMAP), n=262 samples from the temporal cortex (Mayo), and n=337 samples from the frontal pole (MSBB) were processed. The mitochondrial reads of the WGS data were analyzed to estimate the mitochondrial DNA copy number (mtDNAcn) and to detect mitochondrial variants and mutations.

**Metadata:** Individual, biospecimen, and assay metadata for the samples included in this study can be found in the corresponding ROSMAP, MayoRNASeq, and MSBB study metadata files.
