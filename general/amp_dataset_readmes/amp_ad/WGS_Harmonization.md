###The Whole Genome Sequence Harmonization Study (WGS_Harmonization)

This study details an AMP-AD consortium effort to harmonize WGS data generated through grants [U01AG046152](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Projects/DetailsPage?Grant%20Number=U01AG046152), [U01AG046170](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Projects/DetailsPage?Grant%20Number=U01AG046170), and [U01AG046139](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Projects/DetailsPage?Grant%20Number=U01AG046139). The goal of this study is to create a uniformly processed WGS dataset based on donors profiled through the following studies.
* [ROSMAP](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn3219045)
* [MSBB](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn3159438)
* [MayoRNAseq](https://adknowledgeportal.synapse.org/Explore/Studies/DetailsPage?Study=syn5550404)

This study provides data from a joint study analysis where variants were called across the three studies. This data is available in two formats:
* All variant sites from the full large batch are included in the subsetted VCFs, but the allele counts are updated for the study specific batches only.
* Data where where all three studies are included in the VCF.

**Raw data in the form of FASTQ files are available for download from all samples. Previously available BAM files from these samples aligned to the GRCh37 genome build have been archived and are no longer available for download or transfer to an AWS compute instance. **
