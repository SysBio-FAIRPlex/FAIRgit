###The Mayo Clinic LOAD genome-wide association and brain gene expression (MayoLOADGWAS) study
Data are provided for the Mayo Clinic LOAD genome-wide association and brain gene expression studies. All subjects with Mayo Clinic LOAD GWAS data (844 AD cases and 1,255 controls) were selected from two clinical AD Case-Control series: Mayo Clinic Jacksonville (MCJ: 353 AD cases and 331 Controls), Mayo Clinic Rochester (MCR: 291 AD cases and 787 Controls) and a neuropathological series of autopsy-confirmed subjects from the Mayo Clinic Brain Bank (MCBB: 298 AD cases and 223 non-AD Controls). All subjects from the clinical series (MCJ and MCR) were diagnosed by a Mayo Clinic neurologist; all control subjects had a Clinical Dementia Rating score of zero at the most recent time of testing; all LOAD patients had a diagnosis of probable or possible AD according to the NINCDS-ADRDA criteria. Subjects selected from the Mayo Clinic Brain Bank underwent neuropathologic evaluation by Dr. Dennis Dickson. All ADs had definite diagnosis according to the NINCDS-ADRDA criteria and had Braak scores of ≥4.0. All non–AD Controls had Braak scores of ≤2.5; many had brain pathology unrelated to AD and, in particular, many of the non-AD samples used in the brain expression data were selected based on the presence of primary tauopathy (progressive supranuclear palsy).

All of the subjects for the brain expression data included in this data release were from the MCBB cohort and were participants in the Mayo LOAD GWAS. The Mayo Clinic LOAD GWAS PI is Dr. Steven G Younkin and the study is described in detail in [1]. The Mayo Clinic Brain Expression Studies PI is Dr. Nilufer Ertekin-Taner. The Mayo Clinic brain eGWAS from the microarray data is described in detail in [2].
