### The Virus Resilience Lymphoblastoid Cell Line (VirusResilience_LCL) Study

This study provides RNA-seq from 60 lymphoblastoid cell lines selected for carriage of mitochondrial variants that have emerged from analysis of AMP-AD mitochondrial data.

The goal of this study is to generate transcriptomic profiles for the general population carrying susceptible variants after infected with viruses. LCLs were generated from a set of donors that participated in the Harvard Personal Genome Project [(www.pgp.med.harvard.edu)](www.pgp.med.harvard.edu). The mitochondrial variant (chrM:10398, m.10398A>G) was used as a selection criterion for selecting the donor samples. Thirty individuals had the A allele and 30 individuals had the G allele. Three individual donors that have the G allele had also a T allele 2 bases downstream which is a C allele as wildtype. The genotype allele status for the variant was obtained from high coverage whole-genome sequencing data (using Complete Genomics technology) obtained from sequencing the genome of these donors.

LCLs were created by Epstein-Barr virus transformation of PBMCs obtained from whole blood using phytohemagglutinin as a mitogen at Coriell Institute for Medical Research (CIMR).

Donor cells were cultured in RPMI medium supplemented with 10% Fetal Bovine Serum (FBS) and 1% penicillin-streptomycin and maintained at 37°C.
