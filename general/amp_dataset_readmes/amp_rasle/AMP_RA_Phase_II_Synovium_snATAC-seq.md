# AMP RA Phase II Synovium snATAC-seq

Unimodal 10x Genomics Epi ATAC profiling of 18 disaggregated synovium samples from 14 RA and 4 OA cases.

All samples are aliquots of an initial pool of live CD235a- cells sorted from synovial tissue - other aliquots of which were used to collect 3' CITE-seq and multimodal 5' CITE-seq + TCR/BCR repertoire data.

Dataset includes raw fastq files, Cell Ranger output (Cell Ranger ATAC v1.1.0), and standardized assay and biospecimen metadata that conforms to the [ARK Portal Data Model](https://ark-portal.github.io/data_model/).
