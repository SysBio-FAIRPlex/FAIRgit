# AMP RA.SLE Genomic Variants v2

AMP RA/SLE subject SNP genotyping by Illumina Infinium Multi-Ethnic Global BeadChip. Genomic DNA was derived from the buffy coat of whole blood sample centrifuged in heparan sulfate tubes.

These results are derived from an improved processing of the same data used to process the results shared in the [AMP RA/SLE Genomic Variants v1](https://arkportal.synapse.org/Explore/Datasets/DetailsPage?id=38814662) dataset included in the ARK 1.0 release.

Data was processed to remove duplicated samples and genetically duplicated specimens.
