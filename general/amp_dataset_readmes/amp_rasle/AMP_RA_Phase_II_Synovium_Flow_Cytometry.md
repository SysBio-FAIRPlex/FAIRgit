# AMP RA Phase II Synovium Flow Cytometry

Current dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.7303/syn65877428.2

Flow cytometry profiling of 20 disaggregated frozen synovium samples from 5 OA and 15 RA cases using a BD FACSAria Fusion cell sorter with BD FACSDiva 8.0.1 software.

All 20 samples are aliquots of an initial pool of live CD235a- cells sorted from synovial tissue - other aliquots of which were used to collect CITE-seq, snATAC-seq, and multimodal 5' CITE-seq and TCR/BCR repertoire data.

Dataset includes standardized biospecimen metadata that conforms to the [ARK Portal Data Model](https://ark-portal.github.io/data_model/).
