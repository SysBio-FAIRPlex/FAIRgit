# AMP SLE Urine Kiloplex Proteomics

## AMP SLE Phase I Urine Kiloplex Quantibody 1000

As described in [Urine Proteomics and Renal Single-Cell Transcriptomics Implicate Interleukin-16 in Lupus Nephritis](https://doi.org/10.1002/art.42023).

These data include abundances for 1000 protein targets profiled in urine collected from lupus nephritis (LN) patients and seven healthy controls (HC). A total of 36
unique LN patients were profiled at one or more time points starting at the time of the diagnostic renal biopsy and 3, 6, and 12 months after. A total of 21-30 LN
samples are available at any one time point:

Biopsy | 3 months | 6 months | 12 months
30 | 21 | 21 | 30

Protein concentrations reported as pg/mL urine after correction for urine creatinine and normalization against standards

## AMP SLE Phase II Urine Kiloplex Quantibody 1200

As described in [Urine proteomic signatures of histological class, activity, chronicity, and treatment response in lupus nephritis](https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.172569).

These data include abundances for 1200 protein targets profiled in urine from 226 systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE) patients with one or more sample time point
and 10 healthy control samples. The SLE sample time point count is:

Visit_0 | Visit_1 | Visit_2 | Visit_3
225 | 135 | 108 | 95

Concentration of each protein target was normalized by urine creatinine to account for urine dilution. Urine protein abundances are expressed are pg protein/mg creatinine.

### Known Issues

The Phase II clinical data in `Variable dictionary.xlsx` and `ph2_urine_proteomics_basicclinicodemographic.txt` references `prcr` as "Urine protein to creatinine ratio,
numeric". However the publication refers to this metric as `UPCR`.
