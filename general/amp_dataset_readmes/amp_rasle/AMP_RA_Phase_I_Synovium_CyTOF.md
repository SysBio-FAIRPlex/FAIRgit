# AMP RA Phase I Synovium CyTOF

AMP RA Phase I CyTOF of 28 dissociated synovium samples from 26 patients, collected either as biopsies or arthroplasties, stained with a 35 parameter AMP synovial marker panel. Two synovium samples were profiled from two patients, however the synovium collection procedure used for these samples is unknown.

Patient type count:
|**RA** | **OA**|
|:-:|:-:|
|15 | 11|

Synovium Collection Procedure Counts:
|**Arthroplasty** | **Biopsy**|
|:-:|:-:|
|17 | 4|

Both raw and normalized data is available in FCS files. All 28 samples passed initial QC checking that each sample had > 1x10^4 singlet events and < 80% events marked as debris.
