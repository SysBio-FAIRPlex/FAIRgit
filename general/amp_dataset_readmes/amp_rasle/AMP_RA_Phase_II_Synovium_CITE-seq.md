# AMP RA Phase II Synovium CITE-seq

Multimodal 10x Genomics 3' CITE-seq profiling of 85 disaggregated synovium samples from 72 RA and 10 OA cases. Surface protein expression profiling targeted 58 proteins.

All samples are aliquots of an initial pool of live CD235a- cells sorted from synovial tissue - other aliquots of which were used to collect snATAC-seq and multimodal 5' CITE-seq and TCR/BCR repertoire data.

Dataset includes raw fastq files, Cell Ranger output (Cell Ranger v3.1.0), and standardized assay and biospecimen metadata that conforms to the [ARK Portal Data Model](https://ark-portal.github.io/data_model/).
